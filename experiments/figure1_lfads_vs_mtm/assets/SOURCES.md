# Figure assets

- `allen_sagittal_slice_ml_minus2mm.png`: sagittal slice at ML = −2.0 mm of the
  [Allen CCF 50 µm average template](https://download.alleninstitute.org/informatics-archive/current-release/mouse_ccf/average_template/average_template_50.nrrd)
  (Wang et al., 2020). It is the background of panel a. Neurons are placed by their IBL
  anterior–posterior and dorsal–ventral coordinates, projected over the medial–lateral axis.
- `ibl_wheel_task.png`: cropped from Fig. 1a of IBL et al. (2025), Brain-Wide Map. The figure rotates
  it 90° clockwise. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
